Generate Credit Note — Meghraj Express

Enter the credit note number and date manually. The SBI original invoice number, invoice date, service month, SOL, GSTIN and original taxable amount are fetched from the verified PostgreSQL invoice database. Credit-note values are entered per invoice and cannot exceed the remaining uncredited taxable value.

Reference format: Service Entry Modification Details, including Sl. No., SAC Code, Invoice No., Invoice Date, Month and Amount; Sub Total, CGST 9%, SGST 9%, Grand Total, amount in words and Meghraj Express signatory. One credit note may include many invoices.

Safety: The sample source is not automatically imported; historical invoice records not yet verified in the isolated registry cannot be credited. This module performs no financial or GST filing postings.

Invoice No. | Credited Taxable Amount. Original invoice date, month, SAC code and customer data are fetched automatically; do NOT enter those fields manually. Example: MXP18430 [TAB] 100.00. Maximum 1,000 invoice rows.

Enter a credit note number, date and source invoice rows to begin.

Invoice Cancellation / Number Void Register

Cancel a finalized invoice or void a manually allocated but unused MXP number. The invoice number will never be recycled. Finalized invoice and shipment evidence remain immutable; the cancellation reason, reviewer reference, authenticated operator and server timestamp are stored in PostgreSQL.

Safety: Only available in isolated QA until independent GST filing, payment and credit-note checks are integrated. This does not automatically reverse reported tax or reconciled payments.

Check an invoice number first. Cancellation cannot be undone by editing records.

Audit records load on request and are read-only.

Bulk Report — Selected Invoice Numbers

Upload an Excel file containing only one column, Invoice No. Invoice numbers may be in any order. The system looks up exact matches from verified saved invoice records and generates a combined Excel report with full available 53-column details, plus a review tab for missing or conflicting numbers. No reconciliation is performed and no financial records are changed.

Requires an authorized report viewer and a verified invoice database connection.
